# Forked 25-Second Instagram Story Storyboard

## Overview

- Format: 9:16 Vertical Video (1080 x 1920)
- Framerate: 30 fps
- Total Duration: 25.000 seconds (750 frames)
- Musical Tempo: 96 BPM (1 Bar = 75 frames = 2.50s)
- Sound-Off Readability: High-contrast typography and clear visual states allow 100% comprehension without audio.
- Instagram Safe Zones: 180px top buffer, 220px bottom buffer, 72px horizontal margin.

## Sound-Off Design Verification

- **Headline Scanability**: All headlines use short 2 to 4 word phrases readable within 400ms.
- **Color Coding**: Danger and doubt are consistently signified by Terracotta `#b54739`; certainty and clarity by Forest Green `#245d3e`.
- **Motion Continuity**: Visual elements morph and transition with motivated momentum, guiding the eye from top hook down to the simulation results and final URL.
